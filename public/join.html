What You Need

  • A computer that stays on — a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 works, so does an old laptop or a cloud server
  • An internet connection — broadband or better
  • A terminal — you already have one if you're reading this

No account. No sign-up form. No email. No one to ask.

How to Join

  1. Open a terminal on your machine.

  2. Copy and paste this command, then press Enter:

    curl -fsSL https://emergenceinstitute.live/scripts/install_pi_node.sh -o install.sh
    chmod +x install.sh && sudo ./install.sh

    It will download, verify, and start the node software. This takes a minute or two.

  3. Watch the terminal. You should see something like this:

    [STORAGE] Initialized clean Merkle journal database...
    [SWARM] Generating fresh Ed25519 node identity signature...
    [HANDSHAKE] Sending CELL_ANNOUNCE identity frame...
    [HANDSHAKE] [SUCCESS] Registered verified key for peer!

    When you see [SUCCESS], your node is live and connected to the mesh.

You're In

Your node is now a peer on the Lattice. It validates proposals, witnesses certificates, and helps secure the network. Nothing to configure. Nothing to maintain day-to-day.

The mesh is small right now — a handful of nodes run by a handful of people. Every new node makes it stronger. Yours is one of them.

Where is this going?
The Lattice is designed to be a peer-governed network — no central server, no company, no platform. Nodes coordinate directly. What they coordinate about depends on who runs them. Right now it's early. You joining shapes what it becomes.
